How to use Tidy Reel

Clear steps from scan setup to actual deletion.

Tidy Reel helps you identify and organize duplicate decisions, but final deletion still happens in Apple Photos. Use this flow if you want the safest cleanup process.

01 Choose what Tidy Reel can scan

Grant Full Photo Access or Limited Photos access before scanning.

  • With Limited Photos access, Tidy Reel only scans the photos you selected
  • If Limited access is on but no photos are selected, choose photos first
  • You can manage the selected subset later from Settings
02 Set the right sensitivity

Choose a scan level based on how broad you want matching to be.

  • Low: fastest and safest for exact duplicates
  • Medium: balanced for common duplicates with minor edits
  • High / Maximum: broader matching for crops and similar shots, but slower and stricter review needed
03 Review each duplicate group

Open a group and decide what should happen before sorting.

  • Tap the photo you want to keep if you disagree with the recommendation
  • Zoom in to compare sharpness and detail
  • Use Ignore Group if you want to skip that set until the next full rescan
04 Sort into albums

Tidy Reel organizes your decision into two albums after review.

  • Your keep-photo is added to "Tidy Reel Selected"
  • The remaining photos from that group are added to "Tidy Reel Duplicates"
  • This step does not delete photos from your library
05 Actually delete duplicates

Deletion is done in Apple Photos after Tidy Reel finishes organizing.

  • Open Photos → Albums → "Tidy Reel Duplicates"
  • Review those photos again, then delete them in Photos
  • Open Recently Deleted in Photos if you want to permanently remove them right away
06 When to rescan

Run a fresh scan after major photo-library changes.

  • Rescan after deleting, importing, or heavily editing photos
  • Ignored groups come back only after a full Scan Again run
  • A fresh scan gives the most accurate current state
Help topics

The most common Tidy Reel questions, in one place.

These are the core parts of the experience most people ask about: permissions, duplicate matching, choosing a keep-photo, and what actually happens when you sort a group.

Photo access

Tidy Reel supports both Full Photo Access and Limited Photos access.

  • With Limited Access, the app only sees the photos you approved
  • You can manage the approved subset from inside the app
  • You can upgrade to Full Access later from iPhone Settings
Matching sensitivity

Sensitivity changes how broad the duplicate scan should be.

  • Lower settings focus on safer, more obvious duplicate matches
  • Higher settings broaden matching to catch more similar photos
  • Useful for bursts, reframed shots, and lightly edited copies
Review + keep-photo choice

Tidy Reel recommends a best shot, but you can override it.

  • Zoom in to inspect sharpness and detail
  • Tap another photo if you want to keep a different one
  • Your chosen keep-photo stays separate from the app’s recommendation
What “Sort to Albums” does

Tidy Reel does not hide what happened after review.

  • Your chosen photo goes to the selected album
  • The duplicate set goes to the duplicates album
  • Nothing is deleted yet — the albums are the handoff for your final review in Photos
Privacy

Tidy Reel is built around on-device processing.

  • No photo uploads to Gearutil for duplicate detection
  • No account required for core functionality
  • No external service needed to compare your photo library
If something looks wrong

Sometimes the photo library may change during or after a scan.

  • If the library changed, some results may no longer match the current state
  • In that case, run a fresh scan for the most accurate view
  • If you are stuck, email support with what screen you were on
FAQ

Quick answers to the main support questions.

Does Tidy Reel upload my photos?
No. Tidy Reel is designed to process duplicate matching and review on-device. Your photos are not uploaded to Gearutil for duplicate detection.
Can I use Tidy Reel with Limited Photos access?
Yes. If you grant Limited Photos access, Tidy Reel only works with the subset of photos you approved, and you can manage that approved set from the app.
What if I disagree with the auto-picked best photo?
You can override it. Tidy Reel recommends a keep-photo, but the final choice stays with you.
What happens after I tap Sort to Albums?
Tidy Reel sorts your chosen photo into the selected album and the duplicates into the duplicates album, so the outcome remains easy to inspect. It does not delete anything at that step.
How do I actually delete the duplicates?
Open Apple Photos, go to Albums, open "Tidy Reel Duplicates," review those photos, and delete them there. If you want them gone immediately, also clear them from Recently Deleted in Photos.
What if I chose Limited Photos access but did not select any photos?
Choose photos first using the Limited Photos picker or allow more access in Settings. Tidy Reel needs at least one selected photo before it can run a meaningful scan.
What should I send if I contact support?
Include what you were trying to do, whether the issue happened during scan, review, or sorting, and whether you were using Full Photo Access or Limited Photos access.

Still stuck?

Send a short description of the issue and what step you were on. We’ll use that to help you faster and avoid sending you generic answers.
