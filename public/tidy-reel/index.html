Step 01 — Home

Begin with a focused cleanup workflow.

Tidy Reel is designed to feel calm instead of cluttered. The entry screen is there to get you into cleanup fast: scan the library, revisit the previous scan, and move directly into review.

  • Simple launch point with minimal friction
  • Fast return to the latest scan state
  • Settings available without getting in the way
Step 02 — Scan

Find duplicate and near-duplicate photos, not just perfect copies.

Camera rolls usually get messy from bursts, repeated attempts, slightly reframed shots, and quick edits. Tidy Reel is tuned to catch those patterns so the app reflects how people actually take photos.

  • Exact duplicate matching
  • Near-duplicate grouping for similar captures
  • Sensitivity controls for safer or broader cleanup
Step 03 — Results

See grouped results instead of a giant unsorted dump.

Once the scan finishes, Tidy Reel presents grouped duplicates and similar shots in a way that makes decisions faster. You can review one set at a time instead of trying to clean the entire library blindly.

  • Clear duplicate groups
  • Recommended best-shot selection
  • Structured flow from scan to action
Step 04 — Compare

Inspect the details before you decide what stays.

Similar photos often differ in subtle ways: one is sharper, one has a better expression, one preserves more detail. Tidy Reel gives you a compare screen built for that kind of judgment.

  • Pinch-to-zoom and drag while zoomed
  • Double-tap zoom/reset
  • Border stays on the auto-picked image while the checkmark follows your chosen keep-photo
Step 05 — Cleanup

Finish with a cleanup flow that stays understandable.

Tidy Reel helps you move through the final step with clarity. After review, your chosen photo and the duplicate set are sorted into separate albums so the result remains visible and easy to audit.

  • Chosen photo goes to the selected album
  • Duplicates go to the duplicate album
  • Designed to support careful cleanup instead of blind deletion

Privacy-first by design

Your photo library stays on your iPhone.

Tidy Reel was built around a basic principle: private photo cleanup should stay private. The app analyzes similarity and image quality on-device, so your photos are not uploaded to Gearutil or passed through outside AI services.

  • No login, account creation, or backend dependency for core use.
  • No photo uploads or cloud processing pipeline.
  • Supports both Full Photo Access and Limited Photos access.
  • Lets you review every duplicate group before taking action.

Private cleanup. Less clutter. Better control.
