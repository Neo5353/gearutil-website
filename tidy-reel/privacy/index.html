Tidy Reel Privacy Policy

Tidy Reel is designed to process photos on your device. We do not upload your photos to external servers for duplicate detection.

We do not collect personal data, create user accounts, or track users across apps or websites.

Photo Access

Tidy Reel requests access to your photo library only so it can scan for duplicate and near-duplicate photos and support cleanup actions you initiate.

If you grant Limited Access, Tidy Reel only sees the photos you approve.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. Tidy Reel does not maintain its own user billing database.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact listed on the support page.
